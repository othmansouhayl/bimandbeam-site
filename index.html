Our Services

BIM Consultancy and Strategy Development

Offer expert consultancy to help small organizations adopt BIM processes. Develop BIM strategies, workflows that align with project goals, and optimize efficiency across all phases of the project lifecycle.

Point clouds to 3D Revit Model

Transform point cloud data into accurate, detailed 3D Revit models. This service is ideal for renovation, retrofit, or historical preservation projects, where existing site conditions need to be captured and integrated into digital models.

BIM Model Audits and Quality Control

Conduct thorough audits on existing BIM models to ensure compliance with industry standards and project requirements. Provide Power BI dashbords and recommendations for improving model quality, data integrity, and file organization.

Revit Modeling and Coordination

Provide high-quality 3D models using Autodesk Revit for architecture, structural, and MEP disciplines. Ensure coordination between all project stakeholders, detecting and resolving clashes early to minimize costly changes during construction.

CAD Drawings to Revit Models

Transform CAD Drawings into accurate, detailed 3D Revit models. This service is ideal for renovation, retrofit, or historical preservation projects, where existing site conditions need to be captured and integrated into digital models.

Revit Family or Dynamo Script

Request a Revit family or dynamo script according to your need, from simple to complex level of details.
For Revit families, we can do it from CAD drawings, point clouds files or even from simple images.
